# From the Jug to the Tap

A researched plan to replace a 5-gallon jug water-delivery service with under-sink
drinking-water filtration, for a home on Aquarion city water in Simsbury, CT —
hard, groundwater-sourced water with trace uranium in the system's own reports.

**Live site:** https://smark11.github.io/water-filtration-plan/

The page covers:

- What Aquarion's 2025 Simsbury water quality report shows (hardness, uranium, chlorine, PFAS at the EPA limit, one lead action-level exceedance)
- The best mail-in certified-lab baseline tests for city water (Tap Score, Cyclopure PFAS, ETR, NTL, Safe Home) and why a first-draw lead sample matters after service-line replacement
- Under-sink system types compared (tankless RO, tank RO, carbon block, UF, inline) — and why only RO fixes hardness taste and uranium at the tap
- A comparison of seven under-sink RO models with prices and running costs
- Eight verified installers serving the Farmington Valley, with CT license-lookup guidance
- Five-year cost curves: jug delivery vs. DIY RO vs. pro-installed RO
- An action checklist

Researched August 2026 with Claude Code. A plan, not professional advice — it
starts with a certified lab test at the tap.
