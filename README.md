# From the Jug to the Tap

A researched plan to replace a 5-gallon jug water-delivery service with in-house
drinking-water filtration, for a private-well home in Simsbury, CT with very hard
water and a trace of uranium (below the EPA limit) in a past test.

**Live site:** https://smark11.github.io/water-filtration-plan/

The page covers:

- Why uranium shows up in Connecticut bedrock wells, and what the trace level means
- What actually removes uranium (RO, anion exchange) and what doesn't (carbon, softeners)
- The recommended architecture: retest first → whole-house softener → under-sink NSF/ANSI 58 RO with remineralization → licensed install + verification test
- A comparison of seven under-sink RO systems with prices and running costs
- Five-year cost curves: jug delivery vs. DIY RO vs. pro-installed RO
- An action checklist and CT testing resources

Researched August 2026 with Claude Code from CT DPH, UConn Extension, USGS, WQA,
and current retail sources. A plan, not professional advice — it starts with a
fresh certified lab test.
